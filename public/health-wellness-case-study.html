RESULTS SUMMARY

A measurable lead-generation campaign.

Across a campaign duration of 6+ months, Meta Lead Forms generated 6,141+ leads at an average cost per lead of ₹14.32, with ₹87,968+ in ad spend managed.

The supplied campaign data also reports 13.32 Lakh+ reach, 47.28 Lakh+ impressions, 55,217+ link clicks and a ₹1.59 average CPC. Downstream qualified leads, appointments and conversions / sales are identified as estimates.
